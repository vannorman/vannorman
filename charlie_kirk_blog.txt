What do we have to gain by being angry with each other?

And by the way, who DOES gain, when we are angry with each other? I guarantee someone is gaining.

It's hard to talk about disagreeing with someone's political motivations and effects on society after they've been gruesomely assassinated. I didn't even know who Charlie Kirk was before he was murdered, and now that I know who he was, I definitely disagreed with most everything he stood for. But that doesn't really matter to me nearly so much as the fact that he was murdered for having and propagating his opinion, a right and freedom we all fight for and everyone deserves.

"I disapprove of what you say, but I will defend to the death your right to say it."

A political or ideologically motivated assassination just occurred on our shores for the first time since the 60s, and we're all at each other's throats over it. Who cares what he stood for? If you want to argue about his points, fine. They have absolutely nothing to do with the wrongness of what happened. Take up the stage and argue about a point on its own if you want. But don't go for each other's throats over Kirk's ideology. That's what the microphone is for. Make your own case for the points. Tying them to Charlie Kirk's murder or comparing him to George Floyd isn't getting us anywhere.

We all condemn murder and assassination right? Can we just start there, where we agree, and talk about what we do from here? How can we co-create a better world for all of us?
